Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BudsBox use karne ka matlab hai ki aap in terms se sehmat hain. Ye seedhe shabdon me likhi gayi hain taaki koi confusion na rahe.

1. Service kya hai

BudsBox aapki shop ke WhatsApp number par aane wale message ka jawab deta hai aur unhe order me badal deta hai. Ye official WhatsApp Cloud API par chalta hai. WhatsApp Business ka apna policy bhi lagta hai — aapko unke rules follow karne honge.

2. Aapka account

  • Sahi business detail dena aapki zimmedari hai.
  • Apna password aur number safe rakhna aapki zimmedari hai.
  • Ek WhatsApp number ek hi shop se jud sakta hai.
  • Aapki shop ke customers se baat karne ka haq aapke paas hona chahiye.

3. Trial aur plan

  • Nayi shop ko 14 din ka free trial milta hai, bina card.
  • Trial khatam hone par AI reply karna band kar deta hai aur naye order ruk jate hain. Aapka data aur juda hua number safe rehta hai.
  • Payment karte hi wahi number turant chalu ho jata hai — dobara connect karne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Plans: Starter ₹999/mahina, Growth ₹1,999/mahina, Pro ₹3,999/mahina. Yearly lene par 10 mahine ka price lagta hai (2 mahine muft).
  • Har plan me AI replies ki ek monthly limit hoti hai. Limit ke baad bhi customer ko jawab milta rehta hai (AI ki jagah smart matching), number chup nahi hota.

4. Payment aur auto-renew

  • Paisa Razorpay ke through lagta hai. Card/UPI detail BudsBox ke paas nahi aati.
  • Monthly plan me aap ek baar mandate approve karte hain; uske baad har mahine apne aap katta hai.
  • Payment fail ya mandate halt hone par shop suspend ho jati hai (free me chalti nahi rehti). Dobara pay karte hi chalu.
  • Yearly plan ek baar ka payment hai, auto-renew nahi.

5. Kaam karne ka tarika

AI customer ke message ko padh kar order banata hai aur aapke catalog se price match karta hai. AI galti kar sakta hai, isliye har order dashboard par dikhta hai aur aap use confirm ya cancel kar sakte hain. Paisa pehle, phool baad me: jab tak aap order ko "paid" mark nahi karte, use fulfilled nahi kiya ja sakta.

6. Kya nahi karna

  • Spam, fraud ya kanoon ke khilaaf message bhejna.
  • WhatsApp ya Meta ke rules todna (isse number band ho sakta hai).
  • Kisi aur ka data chori karna ya service ko todne ki koshish.

Aisa karne par hum account band kar sakte hain.

7. Service ki seema

Hum BudsBox ko 24×7 chalu rakhne ki poori koshish karte hain, par internet, Meta, ya hosting ki galti se kabhi rukavat aa sakti hai. Aise waqt ke liye humari zimmedari service fee tak seemit hai. BudsBox aapki shop ka malik nahi hai aur aapke customers se hone wale deal ka hissa nahi hai.

8. Account band karna

Aap kabhi bhi apna number disconnect kar sakte hain (ek click) aur account hataane ke liye likh sakte hain. Hum bhi service misuse hone par account band kar sakte hain.
