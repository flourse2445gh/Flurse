Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BudsBox ek software service hai jo flower shops ko unke apne WhatsApp number par customer ke message ka jawab dene aur order banane me madad karti hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaun sa data rakhte hain, kis liye, aur aap use kaise hataa sakte hain.

1. Kaun data ka owner hai

Har shop apne customers ka data apne account me rakhti hai. Aapki shop ke customer, order aur chat messages aapki hain — BudsBox unhe sirf aapke liye process karta hai. Ek shop doosri shop ka data kabhi nahi dekh sakti: har record par uski shop ka id lagi hoti hai aur har query usi par filter hoti hai.

2. Hum kaun sa data rakhte hain

  • Aapka account: business ka naam, owner ka email, phone (optional), aur ek password hash. Password kabhi plain text me save nahi hota.
  • Shop profile: address, khule hone ka time, delivery detail, tone, aur aapka UPI id / payment QR (jo aap khud upload karte hain).
  • Catalog: aapke items aur unke price.
  • Customer aur order: WhatsApp number, naam, message, aur order ki delivery detail (address, date, recipient).
  • Connection: aapke WhatsApp number ki id aur uska access token, taaki reply bhej sakein.

3. Data kis liye use hota hai

  • Customer ke message ka jawab dena aur order banana.
  • Aapko naye order ki jaankari dena (dashboard par badge aur bell).
  • Delivery se ek din pehle reminder bhejna.
  • Subscription aur payment sambhalna.
  • Service ko surakshit rakhna (fraud, misuse rokna).

4. Kiske saath data jaata hai

Hum aapka data bechte nahi hain. Kuch service partners kaam karne ke liye zaroori hain:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API): message bhejne aur lene ke liye — yahi aapka official WhatsApp channel hai.
  • AI providers (jaise Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI): customer ka message reply aur order samajhne ke liye process hota hai.
  • Razorpay: subscription ka paisa lene ke liye. Card/UPI ki detail Razorpay ke paas rehti hai — BudsBox ke server par kabhi nahi aati.
  • Hosting aur database (Vercel, Neon Postgres) aur, agar aap on karein, email provider (Resend).

5. Password aur session

Password scrypt se hash hota hai. Login ke baad ek HttpOnly cookie milti hai; us cookie ka sirf hash database me rehta hai. Password reset link ek hi baar chalta hai aur 1 ghante me expire ho jata hai. Reset karne par purane saare session turant band ho jate hain.

6. Data kitne din rehta hai

Data tab tak rehta hai jab tak aapka account chalta hai. Aap account hataane ke liye likh sakte hain; uske baad hum aapki shop ka data delete kar dete hain. Kuch record (jaise payment ki entry) kanoon ya hisaab ke liye thoda lamba rakhna pad sakta hai.

7. Aapke haq

Aap apna data dekh sakte hain, theek kar sakte hain, export kar sakte hain (operator panel me CSV export hai) aur delete karwa sakte hain. Iske liye neeche diye email par likhein.

8. Contact

Koi bhi sawaal ya data request ke liye: support@budsbox.app

Note: ye policy product ke asli behaviour ke hisaab se likhi gayi hai. Launch se pehle apne business ki legal detail (firm ka naam, address, GST agar ho) jod kar ek baar legal review zaroor karwa lein.
