Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Free trial

Nayi shop ko 14 din ka free trial milta hai, bina koi card ya payment. Trial me paisa lagta hi nahi, isliye refund ka sawaal nahi. Trial khatam hone se pehle aap kuch bhi pay nahi karte.

2. Naya payment — 7 din ki guarantee

Pehli baar koi plan lete hain aur 7 din ke andar lagta hai ki BudsBox aapke kaam ka nahi hai, to hum poora paisa wapas kar dete hain. Bas us 7 din ke andar likh dein. Paisa wahi jagah wapas jaata hai jahan se aaya tha, aur usme 7–10 kaam ke din lag sakte hain (bank/Razorpay ke hisaab se).

3. Renewal payment

Auto-renew ya yearly renewal ke case me, agar charge hone ke 3 din ke andar aap batate hain ki aapko nahi chahiye, to us renewal ka paisa wapas kar dete hain. Uske baad wala period refund nahi hota, kyunki service use ho chuki hoti hai.

4. Refund kab nahi milega

  • 7 din (naya) ya 3 din (renewal) ki window nikal jaane ke baad.
  • Agar account rules todne ki wajah se band kiya gaya ho (spam, fraud).
  • Agar WhatsApp number Meta ki taraf se band ho gaya ho — ye Meta ke haath me hai, BudsBox ke nahi.
  • Agar aapne apni shop se customer ka paisa liya hai — wo aapke aur aapke customer ke beech ka mamla hai, BudsBox usme nahi aata.

5. Auto-renew kaise band karein

Monthly plan me aap ek baar mandate approve karte hain. Use rokne ke liye dashboard me auto-pay band karein ya humein likhein — hum aapka Razorpay subscription cancel kar dete hain, aur aage koi charge nahi hota. Ab tak ka paid period chalta rehta hai.

6. Service band hone par

Agar hum BudsBox band karein, to jitne din ka aapne paisa diya hai aur service nahi mili, us hisse ka refund kar dete hain.

7. Refund kaise maangein

Apni shop ka naam aur payment ki detail (Razorpay payment id ya receipt) ke saath likhein: support@budsbox.app. Hum 2 kaam ke din me jawab dete hain.
